## 🚀 Our Products

### 🪙 [Kham](https://kham.la) — Real-Time Gold Pricing Platform

**Kham** (ຄຳ — means "Gold" in Lao) is the **first real-time gold pricing platform in Laos**, currently serving over **500+ users**. We provide live pricing data with buy/sell rates calculated in the Lao standard unit (**1 Baht = 15 grams of gold**), giving local investors up-to-the-second market information for the first time.

> 🔜 Beyond gold, we have plans to expand into additional asset classes in the near future.
